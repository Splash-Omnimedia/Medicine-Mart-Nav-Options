Website navigation study

Four ways out of a tall menu

Pharmacy Services holds 10 of the site's 26 navigation destinations, each with its own description. That is what makes today's menu tall and text-heavy — at 1440×900 it runs past the fold. Below is where we are now, then four directions, all drawn in the approved brand system. Each one shows the Pharmacy Services menu open, and only that menu. Every existing destination and every existing label is kept in all four. Nothing is renamed, merged or removed.

How to give feedback: scroll through all five, then tell Lauren which option number you'd back and the one thing you'd change about it. If you're torn between two, say which audience each one serves better — someone who knows what they came for, or someone browsing. These are static pictures of one open menu, not working prototypes.

Two things worth deciding alongside the layout

Neither of these changes the visual direction, but both affect whichever option wins.

1. Contact Us duplicates four destinations

The Contact menu's six items point at only three unique pages — four of them already live under other menus. That is a separate cleanup from the layout question, but it would shorten the Contact dropdown in whichever option wins, and it would shorten the current menu too.

2. The three service groupings

A, B and D all sort the ten services into Prescriptions, Health & Prevention and Extra Support. Those names are a starting point, not a recommendation — if the team reads them differently, that is useful to know now rather than after build.

What is already fixed

  • Only the eight approved brand colours appear anywhere in these mockups.
  • The logo is the supplied artwork, unmodified — it is not redrawn anywhere.
  • Every hexagon is a regular hexagon; none are stretched to fit.
  • All copy is the site's existing copy. Descriptions are trimmed in Option A, never rewritten.

One accessibility note, carried over from the site

The small green uppercase labels (Prescriptions, Health & Prevention and so on) use Green 1 on a light ground, which measures 2.75:1 — under the 4.5:1 that small text normally needs. That is the approved site-wide eyebrow treatment, not something introduced here, and it is the same on every page today. It is worth a separate decision; the palette-safe alternative is Pharmacy Blue with a green mark beside it. Every other piece of text on these boards clears AA.
